# Organization marks

All 16 entries were requested by Sergey, who confirmed that this is a list of organizations he worked with. They are images only; no new employment records, job titles, dates or military-service claims have been added. “1800” was corrected to Unit 8200 at his request, and Israel Aerospace Industries was added in his follow-up.

Original downloads are stored in `public/logos`. Published source links and the required CC BY-SA 3.0 credits for the military emblems are in `public/logos/credits.html`, linked from the footer in all three languages. ElOp and Mellanox use their historical brand marks. The Ministry of Finance emblem represents the combined government SIGMA / MERKAVA entry; it is not a newly drawn SIGMA or MERKAVA logo.

Artwork colors, shapes and intrinsic proportions are unchanged. The UI applies individual maximum widths and heights to balance wordmarks and emblems. Logos are served locally, with no third-party image requests. SVG files were checked for script elements, foreign objects and external image dependencies; none were found.

The current implementation uses eight columns on large screens, four on tablets and two on phones. Asset decoding, accessible names, image bounds and horizontal overflow are covered by the existing multilingual browser smoke check. The standalone HTML export also embeds these images.
